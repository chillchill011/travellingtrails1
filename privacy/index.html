JOB CAMPAIGN EXPLORER

Privacy Policy

Effective: 11 October 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Job Campaign Explorer (JCE), operated for private authorized use by Aniket Deshmukh, accesses, uses, stores, and shares information, including Google user data obtained through Google APIs.

2. Information JCE processes

JCE may process candidate profile information, campaign preferences, CV files supplied by the user, job and application records, integration identifiers, operational logs, and data necessary to provide Gmail draft and Telegram notification features.

3. Google user data JCE accesses

When a user connects Gmail through Google OAuth, JCE uses the authorized Gmail connection only for its user-facing draft workflow. Depending on the authorized scopes, JCE may:

  • identify the connected Gmail account;
  • list Gmail drafts for reconciliation and duplicate prevention;
  • read a specific draft needed for integrity/reconciliation checks; and
  • create or update Gmail drafts requested by the JCE workflow.

JCE does not automatically send Gmail messages. JCE does not automatically submit job applications. Users review and manually send any application email.

4. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to provide or maintain the Gmail draft features described on the JCE homepage, including account verification, draft creation, draft update, reconciliation, duplicate prevention, security, and troubleshooting.

Google user data is not used for advertising, credit decisions, data brokerage, or sale. Google user data is not used to train or improve generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

JCE's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage and security

Google OAuth credentials and tokens are kept in the private n8n credential store and are not committed to the JCE Git repositories. JCE stores only the operational records needed for its features, such as provider route keys, draft identifiers, integrity hashes, candidate-scoped records, and audit information. Sensitive runtime configuration and backups are encrypted or access-restricted according to the JCE production hardening procedures.

JCE's production application, database, and n8n service are not made public through this website. This site contains only public product and policy information.

6. Sharing and human access

JCE does not sell Google user data and does not share it with advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers. Data is processed only by the services required to operate the user-facing JCE features, subject to the user's authorization and applicable provider terms.

Human access to private Google-derived content is limited to the authorized user or to situations necessary for security, troubleshooting with the user's permission, or compliance with applicable law.

7. Retention

Operational records are retained only as needed to provide JCE functionality, maintain integrity and auditability, and support encrypted backup and recovery procedures. OAuth credentials remain in n8n until disconnected or removed. A user may revoke the application's Google access at any time from their Google Account permissions.

8. User controls and deletion

Authorized users may disconnect the Gmail credential, revoke Google OAuth access, or request removal of JCE candidate/integration data. Revoking Google access prevents future API access but does not by itself delete records or encrypted backups that must be retained temporarily for security, integrity, or recovery purposes.

9. Changes

This policy will be updated if JCE's Google data practices materially change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

10. Contact

Questions about JCE privacy or Google data use can be sent to aniket.thanage@gmail.com.
