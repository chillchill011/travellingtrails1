LEADGEN OUTREACH AUTOMATION

Privacy Policy

Effective: 11 October 2026

1. Scope and operator

This policy explains how LeadGen Outreach Automation ("LeadGen"), a privately operated business research and supervised email outreach system, processes company contact information, website findings and data from a Gmail account connected by an authorized operator. Questions and requests may be sent to aniket.thanage@gmail.com.

2. Business research and contact information

LeadGen may collect publicly available company names, websites, industry and location details, published business email addresses, contact names or roles where evidenced, technical website measurements, discovery source URLs and evidence. It also records campaign details, draft versions, review decisions, send authorization and delivery events, and records necessary for duplicate detection and suppression.

Company website findings and lead information may be used to prepare relevant outreach drafts for human review. Technical SEO measurements and published business contact details are not the same as Gmail account data.

3. Gmail access and data processed

When the operator authorizes Google OAuth, LeadGen uses Gmail permissions needed for two functions: sending specific operator-authorized messages and reading incoming email replies. Depending on the scopes granted and the implemented operations, LeadGen may:

  • submit a specifically authorized outreach email to Gmail and record provider message identifiers and send status;
  • list recent mailbox messages and read relevant message or thread content and email headers to match replies with previously sent outreach;
  • process the sender address, subject, message identifiers, timestamps and bounded content excerpts to identify interest, rejection, automated responses, or unsubscribe requests; and
  • retain verified reply and opt-out evidence, operational error information, and contact-suppression records needed to honor requests and avoid unwanted or duplicate mail.

Incoming messages not verifiably related to an outreach send may be examined during the matching process. Current processing is designed to avoid saving new unrelated inbox messages as reply records. Some earlier limited unmatched-message metadata or previews may remain pending controlled retention review; they are not treated as campaign replies.

LeadGen does not require permission to modify mailbox labels, delete messages, or access the entire mailbox with the unrestricted mail.google.com scope for its documented send and reply-reading features.

4. Purposes of Google data use

Google user data is used only for the Gmail functionality described above: authorized delivery, provider-status reconciliation, reply matching, response and unsubscribe processing, duplicate prevention, security, operational diagnostics, and authorized operator notifications. A previous draft review or Telegram approval alone is not treated as permission to send email.

LeadGen does not sell Google user data, target advertisements with it, or use it to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models.

5. AI and service providers

LeadGen uses DeepSeek for outreach copy based on selected company and website facts. For otherwise ambiguous replies that have been matched to a verified outgoing message, a limited reply excerpt may be sent to DeepSeek for classification. Deterministic checks are used before this fallback where appropriate. The system does not intentionally send unrelated inbox messages to the AI classifier.

Relevant classifications and limited reply information may also be delivered to the authorized operator using Telegram notifications. Google handles the underlying Gmail API and delivery. These providers process the information necessary for their respective functions under their applicable terms and controls. Google user data is not disclosed to advertisers or data brokers.

LeadGen's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Storage and protection

OAuth credentials are maintained in the private n8n credential store rather than in this public website or the public GitHub files. LeadGen's operational business state, including authorized send evidence, matched replies and suppression records, is held in a private PostgreSQL database. Operational logs and access-restricted backups may retain necessary information to support auditability and recovery.

The database, n8n editor and private Campaign Explorer are not hosted on this information website. Access is restricted to the authorized operator and the services needed for operation, maintenance, security or legally required handling.

7. Retention

LeadGen retains necessary operational records for as long as needed to provide its functions, maintain a reliable contact and suppression history, investigate delivery uncertainty and support security or recovery. A fixed universal deletion period has not been established for all categories. Records unrelated to verified outreach are subject to privacy minimization and controlled review. Backups may persist temporarily after active records are removed.

Opt-out and suppression information may need to be retained to prevent future contact after an unsubscribe request. These records are not reused to send outreach.

8. User rights, revocation and deletion requests

The Gmail account holder may revoke OAuth access from Google Account permissions and may request disconnection of the n8n credential. Revocation prevents future authorized access, but it does not automatically erase previously recorded operational evidence or backups.

For inquiries, correction requests or deletion requests involving information processed by LeadGen, contact aniket.thanage@gmail.com. Requests will be assessed against applicable legal obligations and the need to preserve suppression and send-integrity records.

9. Website and changes

This public site provides disclosure information. It does not itself authenticate Gmail users, collect Gmail messages or operate the private outreach application. The policy may be updated when actual data practices, scopes or processors change; the effective date identifies this version.

10. Contact

Privacy and Google data use questions: aniket.thanage@gmail.com.
