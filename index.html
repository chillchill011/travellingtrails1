LEADGEN OUTREACH AUTOMATION

Private lead research and supervised business outreach.

LeadGen helps an authorized operator discover relevant companies, review public business contact details and website audit findings, prepare personalized outreach drafts, and monitor replies. Gmail messages can be sent only after separate, explicit operator authorization; draft approval alone never starts sending.

What LeadGen does

Company research

Finds prospective companies, collects publicly available business contact evidence, and checks for duplicates and suppressed contacts.

Website review

Measures website and SEO characteristics and turns verified findings into concise observations for a human to assess.

Outreach review

Uses AI-assisted drafting from selected business and website facts. The operator can review, edit or reject a draft. A separate authorization is required before any live Gmail send.

Gmail replies and opt-outs

Uses authorized Gmail access to send specifically authorized messages and read incoming responses. Reply evidence, suppression rules and contact history help prevent inappropriate or duplicate sending.

Google account data

LeadGen uses the connected Gmail account for explicitly authorized outbound email delivery and for identifying relevant incoming replies, including unsubscribe requests. Inbound messages unrelated to verified outreach may be inspected during matching; current processing is designed not to create new unrelated reply records.

Relevant reply metadata or limited excerpts may be shared with the configured operator notification service and, for ambiguous matched replies, an external AI classification service. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used by LeadGen to train generalized AI models.

LeadGen's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See the Privacy Policy for access, handling, retention, sharing, and user controls.

Private application

This website is a public information and privacy disclosure site only. The actual LeadGen platform, its campaign dashboard, database and automation endpoints are private and accessible only to authorized operators. This website does not connect to Gmail or initiate outreach.
